### Guardrails (common mistakes)

- A touch of a level is not a sweep confirmed, a respected array or an inversion. Wait for the close and the reaction.
- One big candle is not displacement unless it breaks something meaningful and leaves a gap.
- An MSS flag alone does not make a setup; the full model needs the draw, the time and the array.
- A Fibonacci 70.5% alone is not OTE; anchors must be the leg that broke structure.
- Any FVG between 10:00 and 11:00 is not automatically a Silver Bullet; it needs the draw.
- Discount does not mean "price will go up". It means "where ICT prefers to buy if the context is bullish".
- Trading days are not calendar days in IPDA lookbacks; crypto runs 24/7 and needs its own convention.
- Do not replace New York time with a fixed UTC offset.
- Do not hide conflicting timeframes; say which one you are following and why.
