### Where topics live
- Foundations, liquidity, premium/discount: Core Content month 01.
- Order blocks, breakers, FVGs and other PD arrays: month 04.
- IPDA data ranges, quarterly shifts, intermarket analysis: month 05.
- Market maker models and weekly ranges: month 07.
- Daily range, CBDR, intraday profiles, killzone day trading: month 08.
- Index futures specifics: month 10.
- Top-down analysis: month 12.
- FVG-centred entries, MSS and the 2022 model: 2022 Mentorship.
- Silver Bullet, macros and newer refinements: later lectures and mentorships (2023 onward). Use `web_fetch` on a playlist to find the exact video.

Playlist titles and numbering are ICT's; if the user asks for an exact episode, use `web_fetch` on the playlist rather than guessing.
